Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about ordering, delivery and pricing.

Is Supply Station the same company as UAC Services?

Yes. UAC Services is now called Supply Station. Same team, same factory in Retreat, Cape Town, new name.

Where can I buy squeegees in Cape Town?

Directly from us. We make our squeegees at our factory in Retreat, Cape Town, and sell them at R35.00 each excl. VAT in five colours: red, blue, black, dark grey and light grey. Order on WhatsApp or call 082 826 1003.

How do I place an order?

Pick your products and quantities in the order builder on this page, then send the list to us on WhatsApp. You can also call 082 826 1003. We confirm the final price and delivery before anything is sent.

Do your prices include VAT?

No. All prices on this site exclude VAT. The order builder adds 15% VAT and shows your estimated total.

Do you deliver?

Yes. We deliver around Cape Town ourselves: free in Zone 1 (about 15km from Retreat) and for a fee in Zone 2 (about 15 to 40km), confirmed when you order. Further out, you book your own courier to collect from us in Retreat.

Do you offer bulk or wholesale pricing?

Yes. Petrol stations get bulk pricing on volume, and resellers can buy at wholesale / trade pricing. WhatsApp us for a bulk quote or our trade price list.

Can you make branded or custom squeegees?

Yes, on request. WhatsApp us with what you need and we'll quote.

What are your business hours?

Monday to Friday 8am to 5pm, Saturday 8am to 1pm. Closed on Sundays.
